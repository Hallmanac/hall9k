# Getting help

- **Questions and how-to:** ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Hallmanac/hall9k/discussions),
  under Q&A.
- **Bugs:** open an issue using the bug report form.
- **Security problems:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never open a public issue for one.

Hall9k is maintained by one person in their own time. There is no support agreement and no
guaranteed response time, though questions and reports are read.
