## Handoff (required — the last thing in your final message)
===body===
Tasks that depend on this one start with what you write here, and nothing else you
learned survives this session. After your summary, end your final message with a
line reading exactly:
===marker===
    {{HandoffMarker}}
===tail===
followed by a short handoff — a few sentences or a handful of bullets, not an essay,
and nothing after it. Cover three things:

- What you actually did, in terms of what now exists that did not before.
- What someone building on this needs to know: the gotcha, the non-obvious shape, the
  thing you would tell them in person to save them an hour.
- What you deliberately left undone, and why — so nobody re-litigates a settled call
  or assumes an omission was an oversight.

Write it for someone with no access to this session. If there is genuinely nothing
worth handing down, say so in one line rather than padding it — an honest "nothing
surprising here" is useful, and invented significance is not.
